FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does pilot training take?

Typically 12 to 18 months depending on weather, progress and licence type (PPL or CPL).

Is the academy approved by DGCA?

Yes, the academy is approved by the Directorate General of Civil Aviation, India.

What medical is required?

A Class II medical first, then Class I for CPL, from DGCA-approved medical examiners.

Do I need flying experience?

No. We train you from the ground up.

Which licences are covered?

Guidance for Private Pilot Licence (PPL), Commercial Pilot Licence (CPL) and optional type ratings.
